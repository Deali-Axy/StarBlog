Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: a polished logo exploration presentation for the real project StarBlog, an independent open-source personal publishing and photography website.
The client explicitly authorizes free creative direction beyond literal brand-name imagery. Design an original memorable identity with the quality of a thoughtful independent design studio.
Format: landscape 4:3, exceptionally clean flat vector-style graphics with crisp contours, generous negative space, no textures or material mockups, no gradients, no lighting, no 3D. Show one single coherent design, not a set of alternatives. Small concept label in upper-left, a large hero mark centered in the upper-middle, the exact wordmark "StarBlog" beneath it. Bottom strip contains a small one-color reproduction on the left and a small rounded-square app-icon reproduction on the right, both reusing precisely the hero symbol. Keep the bottom miniatures secondary. Use only the concept label and wordmark as text; no taglines or explanatory text.
All shapes must be easy to reproduce as true vectors, with a strong silhouette at small size. Avoid stars, sparkles, books, open pages, fountain pens, blog bubbles, orbit imagery, literal cameras, stock logo graphics, corporate clip art and resemblance to an existing famous logo. Do not draw a compass. No watermark.

Concept label (verbatim): "F / MOTE".
Creative direction: a charming, graphic, ORIGINAL small character used as a logo, full of independent-blog personality. A plump irregular inky pebble creature leans gently forward in three-quarter profile, with one tiny curled tuft on its upper back, two very small cream vertical eye-slits grouped toward the front, and two short unequal flat feet. The body is one compact soft solid silhouette, beautifully designed like a memorable small stamp. No arms, no mouth, no outlines, no facial detail other than the two tiny eyes. NOT a cat, ghost, cloud, octopus, game controller, round pink ball, or existing branded mascot. The asymmetry and leaning posture give it a quietly curious, slightly mischievous personality rather than childish cuteness. Use solid deep violet #6250B3 on very pale lilac-white #F4F1FA. Wordmark "StarBlog" is a friendly sturdy rounded sans, carefully drawn, with slightly irregular character but professionally balanced. Bottom-left show the exact mascot in pure black; bottom-right show the exact cream mascot on a violet rounded-square icon. Keep all miniatures geometrically faithful to the same hero character. One simple silhouette, maximum memorability, not an illustration scene.
